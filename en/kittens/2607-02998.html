Introduction

The kitten lives together with their littermates and other adult cats.
A calm and gentle kitten
We speak to the kitten daily, play with them using toys, and gently hold them as part of socialization training so that they become accustomed to human hands and being held.
We are also gradually acclimating the kitten to everyday household sounds such as vacuum cleaners and television, raising them with consideration so that they can settle into a new home more easily.

About adopting from us (information common to every kitten)

Daily care

The kittens are currently eating dry kibble. They can have their nails trimmed and be shampooed. They stay calm and do not run away even when a dryer is used. Litter training is provided. They can also drink water from a water dispenser. This can be reassuring even for first-time cat owners.

New Home Starter Set

For kittens going to their new families we have prepared a New Home Starter Set. It is a small gift containing useful everyday items such as cat food, toys, nail clippers and used cat litter. We offer it with care so that life in the new environment can begin as smoothly as possible.

Viewing hours

By appointment only (weekdays, weekends and public holidays; about 30–60 minutes)

Viewing location

Viewings are held at 2-6-23 Higashinakahama, Joto-ku, Osaka (nearest station: Midoribashi). By appointment only.

Before you visit

Please avoid contact with other animals on the day of your viewing (this can cause infectious disease). Please limit the viewing party to two people. Please wear a mask during the viewing.

About allergies

Our cattery also has cat breeds other than Siberians, as well as small animals. If you have animal allergies, please be sure to let us know in advance when making a reservation. To help you view with as much peace of mind as possible, we clean in advance and prepare a viewing space without contact with other animals. Allergic reactions differ from person to person.

Going home

The kitten will be handed over after its health condition is stable, after the first vaccination, and with a microchip already fitted. Depending on the kitten’s growth, circumstances and physical condition, the handover date may be extended.

Please note

After a viewing, it is fine if you decide not to take the kitten home because of differences from your expectations, compatibility, or similar reasons. Please make sure you have confirmed and understood your family’s agreement, any allergies, and the transfer fee before visiting.
